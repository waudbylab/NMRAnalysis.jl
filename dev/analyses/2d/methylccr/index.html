Methyl CCR (S²τc)

methylccr2d measures the order parameter of the methyl symmetry axis, as $S^2\tau_c$, from ¹H–¹H cross-correlated relaxation in a methyl group (Sun, Kay and Tugarinov, J. Phys. Chem. B 115, 14878 (2011)). A buildup series $I_a$ and a decay series $I_b$ are recorded at a series of delays $T$, and their ratio is fitted to

\[\left|\frac{I_a}{I_b}\right| = \frac{C\,\eta\,\tanh\!\left(\sqrt{\eta^2+\delta^2}\,T\right)} {\sqrt{\eta^2+\delta^2} - \delta\,\tanh\!\left(\sqrt{\eta^2+\delta^2}\,T\right)},\]

where $\eta$ is the CCR rate, $\delta < 0$ couples the fast- and slow-relaxing coherences, and $C$ is 3/4 for the triple-quantum experiment and 1/2 for the double-quantum one. For an ideal methyl geometry with $r_{HH} = 1.813$ Å,

\[S^2\tau_c = \frac{\eta}{K}, \qquad K = \frac{9}{40}\left(\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\right)^2 \frac{\gamma_H^4\,\hbar^2}{r_{HH}^6} \approx 3.61\times10^{9}\ \text{s}^{-2},\]

so $S^2\tau_c$ in ns is about $0.277\,\eta$, with $\eta$ in s⁻¹.

Usage

Give the decay series, then the buildup:

using NMRAnalysis
methylccr2d(12, 11)
methylccr2d(12, 11; T=[0.002, 0.006, 0.012, 0.02, 0.03], C=1/2)   # double quantum

The delays are read from the buildup series' annotations or vdlist, or asked for. Give them in seconds with T.

Example

The triple-quantum experiment on ILV-labelled FLN5:

decay, buildup = example("methylccr2d_fln5")
methylccr2d(decay, buildup)
